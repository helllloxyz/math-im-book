The fundamental connection between a function's behavior on a surface and its boundary is captured elegantly in the generalized Stokes' Theorem. We begin by considering a differentiable k-form ω on a compact n-dimensional manifold M with boundary ∂M.

∫M dω = ∫∂M ω

Equation 1.1: General Stokes Identity

This identity effectively translates the "derivative" of a volume element into a "flow" through its boundary. If we specialize this to R³, we recover the classical Divergence and Curl theorems.
